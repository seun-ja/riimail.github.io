# Security

Security problems should be reported privately so maintainers have an opportunity to understand and fix them before details become public.

## Report a vulnerability

Do **not** open a public issue or pull request for a suspected vulnerability. Email the maintainer at [hello@oluseun.dev](mailto:hello@oluseun.dev).

Include as much of the following as you safely can:

- the kind of vulnerability you found;
- the affected RiiMail version or commit;
- the conditions and steps needed to reproduce it;
- its likely impact;
- a minimal proof of concept, screenshot, or sanitized log.

Never include real credentials or private mailbox content. Use a dedicated test account when reproducing authentication or mail-handling problems.

## Response process

The project aims to acknowledge a report within three days and provide an estimated timeline for a fix. Once a correction is available, the reporter may be credited in release notes unless they prefer to remain anonymous.

Thank you for helping keep RiiMail and its users safe.
